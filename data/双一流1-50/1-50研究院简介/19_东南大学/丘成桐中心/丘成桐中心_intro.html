The Shing-Tung Yau Center of Southeast University (SEUYC) was established as a new type of scientific research institution of Southeast University on the July 7th, 2017 and was transformed into a special academic zone on the May 10th, 2021. The center is directed by Prof. Shing-Tung Yau, an internationally renowned mathematician and recipient of the Fields Medal, Wolf Prize and Shaw Prize.

Professional talents are the basis of discipline construction. SEUYC takes on the burden of recruiting high level talents and fostering a new generation of world-leading mathematicians. In the past years, SEUYC has introduced 36 full-time researchers (including 7 foreign nationalities), 5 of whom are selected in the national talent programs. Currently, the center has 10 postdoctoral fellows and 44 graduate students.

SEUYC has multiple research directions, covering basic research fields such as differential geometry, algebraic geometry, algebraic number theory, partial differential equations, mathematical physics, theoretical physics, as well as applied research areas including computational mathematics, information science, data science and medical imaging. In recent years, SEUYC members have led over 40 research projects funded by national, provincial, and ministerial-level institutions, as well as overseas young talent program. In the past years, 180 academic papers were published in internationally renowned mathematics and interdisciplinary journals, such as Duke Math. J., Advances in Mathematics, Mathematische Annalen, Crelle's Journal, CVPDE, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. and Inverse Probl., and top-tier physics journal, such as PRL, Scipost Physics and JHEP. The center organizes various academic seminars and small-scale academic conferences throughout the year, with over 70 seminars per year in average.

Based on the research needs and discipline construction requirements, SEUYC focuses on hiring high level talents in the field of fundamental sciences. With the support of the Southeast University and relevant departments, SEUYC adopts flexible and special policies to provide Ample opportunities for development.


东南大学丘成桐中心(简称SEUYC)于2017年7月7日正式揭牌成立，是学校直接管理的“行政虚体、科研实体”的新型科研机构。2021年5月10日，东南大学丘成桐中心由新型科研机构调整为学术特区，由国际数学大师、菲尔兹奖、沃尔夫奖、邵逸夫奖获得者丘成桐院士担任中心主任。

人才为学科建设之基。丘中心以高水平人才引进、造就新一代世界领先数学家为根本任务。过去数年间，中心已引进全日制教师36人（含外籍7人），其中入选国家级人才计划5人。目前，在站博士后10人，在学研究生44人。

丘中心目前有多个研究方向，涵盖了微分几何、代数几何、代数数论、偏微分方程、数学物理、理论物理等基础研究领域和计算数学、信息科学、数据科学、医学影像等应用研究领域。近几年，中心成员先后主持过国家级、省部级、科技部重大等科研项目40余项，在国际高水平数学及交叉学科综合期刊如Duke Math. J., Advances in Mathematics, Mathematische Annalen, Crelle's Journal, CVPDE, SIAM J. Sci. Comput.和Inverse Probl.等杂志，和国际高水平物理期刊如PRL, Scipost Physics, JHEP等上，近年来发表论文共计180篇。中心常年举办各类学术报告会和小型学术研讨会议，每年平均举办约70场。

根据中心目前研究工作的需要和学科建设的要求，丘中心将重点引进基础科学领域的高端人才，依托学校和相关院系的支持，采取灵活特殊的支持政策，为人才的发展提供广阔的空间。东南大学丘成桐中心诚邀海内外英才加盟，共谋发展。
