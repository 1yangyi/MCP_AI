医院简介

河南省儿童医院郑州儿童医院始建于1960年，是三级甲等儿童医院、国家儿童区域医疗中心、河南省儿童医学中心、北京儿童医院郑州医院、郑州大学附属儿童医院，国家建立健全现代医院管理制度试点医院。

Henan Children's Hospital Zhengzhou Children’s Hospital, established in 1960, is a 3A pediatric hospital. It has been approved as the National Children's Regional Medical Center, Henan Children's Medical Center, Zhengzhou Hospital, Beijing Children’s Hospital, and Children's Hospital Affiliated of Zhengzhou University, and has been included by the state as a pilot hospital for the establishment and completion of modern hospital management system.

医院拥有郑东院区（主院区）、东三街院区、南院区和西院区，“一院四区”各有重点、协同发展。核定床位2000张，现有职工约3000余人，其中博士、硕士800余人，高级职称400余人，博士、硕士研究生导师60余人。2024年门急诊量228万余人次，出院病人9万余人次，手术量2万余台次，拥有国家、省市政府特殊津贴专家、省市学术技术带头人、省特聘研究员等近50人。柔性引进国内外知名学科团队10余个。

The hospital has Zhengdong Hospital Area (main hospital area), East Third Street Hospital Area, South Hospital Area and West Hospital Area. "One Hospital and Four Areas" have their own priorities and coordinated development. There are 2000 open beds and more than 3000 employees, including , 800doctors and masters, 400 senior professional titles and 60 doctor and master's supervisors. In 2024, there were 2,280,000 outpatient and emergency patients, 90000 discharged patients and 20000 surgeries. It introduced 50 experts with special allowances from the national, provincial and municipal governments, provincial and municipal academic and technical leaders, and provincial special researchers. It also flexibly brought in 10 well-known domestic and abroad discipline teams.

医院拥有国家临床重点专科建设项目3个（儿科新生儿、儿科重症、小儿外科），省市医学重点（培育）学科44个。拥有省级及以上医教研平台50余个，其中获批国家呼吸系统疾病临床医学研究中心河南研究中心、国家卫健委儿童脑死亡判定质控合格医院、全国罕见病诊疗协作网成员单位、河南省儿科医疗质控中心等，是国家住院医师规范化培训基地、国家新生儿围产期医学专科医师规范化培训基地、中国医师协会基本消化内镜医师培训基地等。拥有国家人社部博士后科研工作站、河南省儿童遗传代谢性疾病重点实验室、河南省儿科疾病临床医学研究中心等科技创新平台28个。

It has 2 national key clinical specialties construction projects of Neonatal and Critical Care Medicine, 40 provincial and municipal key (cultivation) disciplines, over 50 provincial and higher level platforms, among them are National Clinical Research Center for Respiratory Diseases (Henan), Qualified Hospital for Quality Control of Children's Brain Death by the National Health Commission, Member units of the National Rare Disease Diagnosis and Treatment Network,Henan Pediatric Medical Quality Control Center, Henan Children's Emergency Center. It also undertakes series teaching platforms as well, such as National Resident Physician Standardized Training Base, National Standardized Training Base for Neonatal and Perinatal Specialists, and Chinese Medical Doctor Association Basic Gastrointestinal Endoscopy Physician Training Base. Moreover, it has 28 technology innovation platforms including Postdoctoral Programme of National Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, Henan Provincial Key Laboratory of Genetic&Metabolic Diseases, and Henan Clinical Medical Research Center for Pediatric Diseases.

近五年，医院获批科研立项703项（其中国自然基金委及科技部立项21项），科研获奖72项（其中省部级6项），国家授权专利1913项。发表论文5079篇，其中SCI论文489篇、中华及中文核心期刊论文837篇、影响因子＞5分177篇，在世界医学顶级期刊《NEJM》《JAMA》《BMJ》发表论文影响因子最高158.5。

In the past five years, the hospital has been approved 703 scientific research projects (including 21 projects approved by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Ministry of Science and Technology), 72 scientific research awards (including 6 provincial and ministerial awards), and 1,913 state-authorized patents. 5,079 papers were published, including 489 SCI papers, 837 Chinese and Chinese core journal papers, and 177 papers with impact factors>5 points. The highest impact factor of papers published in the world's top medical journals NEJM, JAMA and BMJ was 158.5 points.

医院是中国医院协会儿童医院分会副主委单位、福棠儿童医学发展研究中心副理事长单位、河南省医院协会儿童医院（科）分会主委单位、河南省医院协会儿童医院（科）分会护理专委会主委单位、河南儿科医疗联盟理事长单位（成员单位254家），持续弘扬“河南儿科是一家”理念，合作共赢、错位发展。

The hospital is the vice chairman unit of the Children's Hospital Branch of China Hospital Association, the vice chairman unit of the Futang Research Center of Pediatric Development, the chairman unit of the Children's Hospital (Division) Branch of the Henan Provincial Hospital Association, and the chairman unit of the Henan Pediatric Medical Union. Driven by the concept of "Unite the Henan Pediatrics ", 254 member units have developed in dislocation on the basis of win-win cooperation.

医院位列2023年中国医院科技量值(儿科学)排行榜综合榜全国第19名、儿童医院第11名、全省第1名。荣获全国改善医疗服务行动计划先进典型、省卫生健康系统群众满意医院、省进一步提升服务能力标杆医院、省医改工作先进集体等多项荣誉。

In 2023, the hospital ranks 19th in China’s Hospital Science and Technology (Pediatrics) Ranking for National rank list, 11th in Children's Hospitals rank list and 1st in Henan Province rank list. The hospital has also received a great number of awards and titles, including the “advanced model of the National Medical Service Improvement Plan”, the “public satisfaction hospital”in provincial health system, “example for the further improvement of medical service ability”“advanced group in medical and administrative management”“hospital with excellent performance in women and children health” at the provincial level and so on.
