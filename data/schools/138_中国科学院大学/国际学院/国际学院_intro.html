About IC-UCAS

Introduction

As China’s largest educational institution for graduate education, University of China Academic of Science (UCAS) has actively involved itself in the tide of China’s educational internationalization. The establishment of International College aims at taking the advantages of the educational resources of China Academic of Science (CAS) to boost the development of the university’s internationalization, and attract excellent overseas students to this multinational academic atmosphere.
