学院简介

青岛软件学院、计算机科学与技术学院其前身为创建于1984年的计算机科学系，2001年撤系建院,成立计算机与通信工程学院，2019年院部调整更名为计算机科学与技术学院。经过近40年的不懈努力，已发展成为石油石化行业领先、国内具有较高知名度的IT科技创新和人才培养基地。2020年，为深入贯彻国家软件发展战略，满足国家能源安全战略及青岛市经济社会发展对高端人才的需求，中国石油大学（华东）与青岛市政府、中国石油集团东方地球物理勘探有限责任公司、东软集团有限公司、海信集团有限公司共建青岛软件学院，并于2021年12月获批国家特色化示范性软件学院，从此开启崭新的发展阶段。

学院学科发展迅速，计算机科学进入ESI全球排名第258名（2025年3月），排名前3.03‰。设有软件工程学科一级博士点，计算机技术与资源信息工程二级博士点，计算机科学与技术、软件工程2个硕士学位授权一级学科，拥有资源信息工程博士专业学位授权领域，计算机技术、软件工程2个硕士专业学位授权领域。其中，计算机应用技术为山东省重点学科。学院参与共建化学品安全全国重点实验室、深层油气全国重点实验室；建有海洋物探及勘探开发装备国家工程研究中心-综合环境数字化与模拟分中心、智能油田教育部工程研究中心、山东省智能油气工业软件重点实验室、石油工业软件智能技术山东省高校重点实验室、山东省能源工业大数据发展创新实验室、青岛市随钻仪器及信息处理工程技术研究中心、青岛市油气物联网与人工智能技术工程研究中心、青岛市大型石油工业软件工程研究中心等多层次、体系化的高端科研实验平台。形成软件工程理论、软件工程技术、油气领域软件服务工程、智能信息处理、网络与服务计算、图形图像与可视化、数据科学与信息系统等特色鲜明的学科方向，积累了大量优秀科研成果。近五年，获得教育部科技进步二等奖2项、山东省科技进步二等奖1项、青岛市科技进步一等奖1项，中国计算机学会科技进步杰出奖1项，其他省部级科研奖励5项，在服务于国家能源战略和山东省、青岛市区域经济发展方面发挥了重要作用。

拥有一支高学历、高水平、经验丰富的师资队伍。现有教职工122人，其中教授21人，博士生导师16人，副教授53人，具有博士学位人员64人。有国家级教育部长江学者讲席学者1人，国家级人才2人，中国科协青年人才托举工程1人；省部级泰山产业领军人才1人，泰山特聘专家1人，泰山青年专家4人，山东省优青1人，山东省青年科技奖2人；青岛市青年科技奖4人，青岛西海岸新区高层次领军人才1人，青岛西海岸新区高层次拔尖人才1人，青岛西海岸新区优秀青年人才2人。

人才培养体系完整，设有计算机科学与技术、软件工程、智能科学与技术、人工智能四个本科专业。其中，计算机科学与技术专业入选国家级一流专业建设点，为山东省特色专业；软件工程专业入选国家级一流本科专业建设点，为山东省“卓越工程师教育培养计划”建设专业。以立德树人为根本，通过持续综合改革不断提升人才培养质量。建院以来为国家输送了7100余名（其中，本科生5800余名，硕士生1300余名）具有较高科学素养、较强创新意识和实践能力的复合型高级工程技术人才。

近五年，学院本科和硕士毕业生初次就业率均位居学校各专业前列，就业层次显著提升。目前在读本科学生1474人，研究生739人。学院建有软件工程、计算机系统开发、人工智能、计算机网络、工业互联网等专业实验室20余个，大学生创新实验室8个。实验室占地面积2700平方米，固定资产2960万元，教学设备2580台套。学院重视与国内外著名企业合作加强人才协同培养，近五年获批教育部产学合作协同育人项目60余项，与中国石油、中国石化、中国海油、国家管网、东方物探、海信、东软、华为、百度、浪潮、青软、软通动力、昆仑数智、科大讯飞等数十家企业开展了全方位合作。

“格物计算，致知力行”。学院正致力于打造特色化的发展和育人文化，打造“三支队伍”，落实科教、产教和学科三个融合，建设“五大平台”，持续探索信息与计算的科学世界，培养高端人才，服务国家战略需求、行业与区域发展需要，推动社会发展。

Qingdao Institute of Software, Collegeof Computer Science and Technology was formerly known as the Department of Computer Science founded in 1984, and in 2001, the Department was abolished to establish the School of Computer and Communication Engineering, and in 2019, the Department was renamed as the School of Computer Science and Technology. After nearly 40 years of unremitting efforts, it has developed into a leading IT technology innovation and talent training base in the petroleum and petrochemical industry and has a high reputation in China. In 2020, in order to further implement the national software development strategy, meet the national energy security strategy and the demand for high-end talents for the construction of Qingdao's industrial Internet capital, China University of Petroleum (East China) and Qingdao Municipal Government, China National Petroleum Group Dongfang Ltd., Neusoft Group Co., Ltd. and Hisense Group Co., Ltd. to establish the Qingdao Institute of Software, which was approved as one of the first batch of national specialised and exemplary software institute in December 2021, starting a new stage of development.

The discipline is developing rapidly, with computer science entering the ESI global ranking of 307th (September 2023) and ranking in the top 3.81‰. It has 1 first-level docotral program in software engineering, 2 interdisciplinary doctoral programs in advanced science and engineering computing and oil and gas artificial intelligence, 2 secondary doctoral programs in computer technology and resource information engineering, 2 master's degree authorized disciplines in computer science and technology and software engineering, and 2 master's degree authorized fields in resource information engineering and computer technology and software engineering. Among them, computer application technology is a key discipline in Shandong Province. The college has built a multi-level and systematic high-end research and experimental platform, such as Ministry of Education Engineering Research Center for Intelligent Oilfields, Shandong Provincial Key Laboratory of Intelligent Oil & Gas Industrial Software, Shandong Energy Industry Big Data Development Innovation Laboratory, Qingdao Engineering Research Centre for Drilling Instruments and Information Processing, Qingdao Engineering Research Centre for Oil and Gas Internet of Things and Artificial Intelligence Technology, Qingdao Software Engineering Research Centre for Large Scale Petroleum Industry, and National Engineering Laboratory for Marine Physical Exploration and Exploration Equipment Integrated Environment Digitalization and Simulation Sub-Laboratory. The university has also established a multi-level and systematic high-end research laboratory platform. It has formed distinctive disciplines such as intelligent information processing, network and service computing, graphic image and visualization, data science and information system, software engineering theory, software engineering technology and software service engineering in the oil and gas field, and has accumulated a lot of excellent scientific research results. During the 13th Five-Year Plan period, it has won two Second Prize of Scientific and Technological Progress of the Ministry of Education, one Second Prize of Scientific and Technological Progress of Shandong Province, one First Prize of Scientific and Technological Progress of Qingdao, one Outstanding Prize of Scientific and Technological Progress of the Chinese Computer Society, and five other provincial and ministerial scientific research awards, playing an important role in serving the national energy strategy and the regional economic development of Shandong Province and Qingdao City.

It has a highly educated, high-level and experienced faculty. There are more than 120 faculty members, including 21 professors, 16 doctoral supervisors, 53 associate professors and 64 PhD holders. There are 1 national-level Chang Jiang Scholar Chair Professor by the Ministry of Education, 2 national top talent, 1 Youth Talent Support Program by China Association for Science and Technology, 1 Taishan leading talent, 1 Taishan Distinguished Expert, 4 Taishan young scholar, 1 Shandong outstanding youth, 2 Shandong young science and technology awards, 4 Qingdao young science and technology award, 1 Qingdao West Coast New Area high-level leading talents, 1 Qingdao West Coast New Area high-level top talent, and 2 Qingdao West Coast New Area excellent young talents. In the past five years, the university has undertaken 239 vertical and horizontal research projects, including more than 30 national research projects, such as the National Key Research and Development Program, and more than 70 provincial and ministerial projects, with a total funding of RMB 160 million. More than 600 academic papers have been published in important academic journals and conferences at home and abroad, including more than 200 SCI-indexed papers, 300 EI-indexed papers and 9 ESI highly cited papers; 202 invention patents have been declared, 75 invention patents have been authorized and 112 software copyrights have been issued. The School has developed a series of software platforms and embedded devices in the petroleum field with completely independent intellectual property rights, which have a great influence in the petroleum and petrochemical industry at home and abroad.

With a complete talent cultivation system, there are four undergraduate majors in Computer Science and Technology, Software Engineering, Internet of Things Engineering and Intelligent Science and Technology. Among them, the major of Computer Science and Technology has been selected as a national first-class major construction point and a special major of Shandong Province; the major of Software Engineering has been selected as a national first-class undergraduate major construction point and is a major of the provincial "Excellent Engineer Education and Training Program" construction. With the foundation of moral education, the quality of talent training has been continuously improved through continuous comprehensive reform. Since its establishment, the College has delivered more than 7,100 senior engineering talents with high scientific literacy, strong innovation consciousness and practical ability to the country (including more than 5,800 undergraduates and 1,300 master students).

In the past five years, the initial employment rate of both undergraduate and master's graduates ranked the highest among all majors in the university, and the employment level was significantly improved. At present, there are more than 1474 undergraduate students and 739 postgraduate students. The College has built 20 professional laboratories such as computer network, software engineering and Internet of Things engineering, and 8 innovation laboratories for university students. The laboratories cover an area of 2700 square meters, with fixed assets of 29.6 million RMB and 2580 sets of teaching equipment. The college attaches importance to cooperation with famous enterprises at home and abroad to strengthen the collaborative training of talents. In the past five years, the college has been awarded more than 60 collaborative education projects by the Ministry of Education, and has carried out comprehensive cooperation with dozens of enterprises, such as Hisense, Neusoft, Dongfang Geophysical, Huawei, Baidu, Inspur, Qingsoft, Softcom Power, iFlytek, IBM, Siemens, Cisco, Microsoft, Lucent.

The College is committed to building a distinctive development and nurturing culture, creating "three teams", implementing the integration of science education, industry education and disciplines, and building "five platforms" to continue exploring the scientific world of information and computing, cultivating high-end talents, serving national strategic needs, industry and regional development needs, and serving the needs of the country. We will continue to explore the scientific world of information and computing, cultivate high-end talents, serve national strategic needs, industry and regional development needs, and promote social development.
