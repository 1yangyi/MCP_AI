南方科技大学


南方科技大学艺术中心（简称：南科大艺术中心）成立于2016年8月。中心成立以来，始终围绕南方科技大学“明德求是 日新自强”的校训与建成世界一流研究型大学的宏伟目标，以建成国际化高水平理工科大学艺术中心为愿景，以培养具有卓越审美品位与人文艺术修养的拔尖一流创新人才为使命。


南科大艺术中心致力于打造融合艺术公共课程、艺术团建设与品牌校园文化活动为一体的理工科高校美育教育体系。艺术中心开设全面涵盖一级艺术学科门类下各专业方向课程，每学年常设全校性艺术理论类课程30余门；建设理工科大学高水平艺术团和各艺术门类的学生社团20余个，在实践中激发学生想象力、创造力、表现力，提高审美能力，提升综合素质，促进全面发展的同时，助力理工科学生专业学习精益求精。同时，艺术中心致力于打造理工科大学校园文化和美育教育教学模式，开展校园文化活动，将美育教育教学工作融入到校园文化建设中去，营造浸润式校园文化氛围。持续开展“一季一品”、“南科大文化艺术节”等具有观赏性、艺术性、教育性、参与性的校园文化活动，彰显科技与艺术融合的校园文化特色，让艺术之美惠及全体师生。

 

未来，南科大艺术中心将继续结合南方科技大学“创知、创新、创业”的办学特色，持续探索构建以课程与教材体系为重点，以推进美育教育教学、科研互动与人才培养结合为牵动，以教学与教研并行、科学与艺术融合为抓手的理工科大学美育教育教学模式。




Established in August 2016, the Arts Center of Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTECH Arts Center) has always focused on the SUSTECH motto of “Virtue, Truth, Advance” and the great goal of Southern University of Science and Technology to be a world-class research university, with its vision to build an international high-level university art center of science and technology, aiming to cultivate top innovative talents with excellent aesthetic taste and cultural and artistic accomplishment.


The SUSTECH Arts Center is dedicated to building an aesthetic education system in the science and technology university, which integrates art public curriculum, art group construction and brand campus culture activities. The Arts Center offers a comprehensive course covering all majors under the first-class arts disciplines, with more than 30 academic art theory courses for all students each academic year. More than 20 high-level art troupes and student associations of various art categories in the science and technology university were established, inspiring the students’ imagination, creativity, expressiveness, and improving aesthetic ability, comprehensive quality. While promoting comprehensive development, the Arts Center helps science and technology students optimize their professional learning. Furthermore, the Arts Center is committed to creating the university campus culture and aesthetic education teaching model, carrying out campus culture activities, integrating aesthetic education teaching into campus culture construction, and creating an infiltration campus culture atmosphere. The campus cultural activities, such as “one-piece-a-season”, “cultural festival of South University of Science and Technology”, which are ornamental, artistic, educational and participatory, highlighting the campus cultural characteristics of the integration of science and technology and art, so that the beauty of art will benefit all teachers and students.


In the future, the SUSTECH Arts Center will continue to explore and construct an aesthetic education teaching model in science and technology universities, combining the school-running characteristics of Southern University of Science and Technology, which is “creation for perception, innovation and entrepreneurship”, focusing on curriculum and teaching material system, promoting the combination of aesthetic education teaching, scientific research interaction and talent training, and complying with the idea of integration of teaching, researching, science and art.

     教研机构

 

     美育高等研究中心


     负责人：刘辉（南方科技大学艺术中心主任、讲席教授）


     负责人简介：博士研究生导师，国家级教学名师，著名男高音歌唱家，音乐教育家。

     

     机构简介：

   “高等美育研究中心”致力于研究以“德、智、体、美、劳”为核心的高等院校五育并举的美育体系；研究在培养创新型拔尖人才过程中的美育工作的内容、规律和特点，挖掘和利用学校一切可利用的资源，调动学校一切可调动的因素，以艺术通识课为基本内容，深入研究高等院校美育工作的内涵与外延；研究美育工作在培养学生高尚的人格、高贵的品质、高雅的气质、高格的追求中的的作用和意义；研究美育工作在培养具有全面素养的拔尖人才、在推动人类文明进步和社会发展中的根本作用，从而探索构建一套适用于高等院校美育工作、具有领先意义和普惠价值的教育体系。





     Center for Higher Aesthetic Education Research

     Responsible person: Liu Hui (Director of the SUSTECH Arts Center, Chair Professor of the Southern University of Science and Technology)

     Brief introduction to the responsible person: tutor of doctoral degree, national-level famous teacher, famous tenor, music educator.

     Center introduction:

   "Center for Higher Aesthetic Education Research" is dedicated to studying the system of aesthetic education in higher education institutions with "moral, intellectual, physical, aesthetic and labor" as the core; focusing on the content, laws and characteristics of aesthetic education in the process of cultivating innovative and outstanding talents, exploring and utilizing all available resources and mobilize all available factors in the school, and studying the connotation and extension of aesthetic education in higher education institutions with the basic content of general arts courses; studying the role and significance of aesthetic education in cultivating students' noble personality, noble quality, elegant temperament and high quality pursuit; studying the fundamental role of aesthetic education in cultivating outstanding talents with comprehensive literacy and in promoting the progress of human civilization and social development., so as to explore the construction of a set of education system with leading significance and universal value applicable to the aesthetic education in higher education institutions.
