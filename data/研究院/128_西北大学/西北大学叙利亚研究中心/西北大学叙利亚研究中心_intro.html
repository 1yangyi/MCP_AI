叙利亚研究中心概况

发布时间：20-06-16    浏览：4265  

一、中心概况

中心简介：

Introduction：

西北大学叙利亚研究中心2017年7月获批教育部国别和区域研究备案中心，10月13日正式挂牌成立，是迄今为止全国唯一的叙利亚问题研究机构。叙利亚研究中心的性质与定位是：构建以学科建设、学术研究、国际交流、智库功能、人才培养、社会服务等为基本内涵的高端、共享、开放的平台，对叙利亚问题又不限于叙利亚问题进行协同合作研究；致力于建设中国叙利亚问题高端智库，为中国与叙利亚及中东国家的交流交往提供学术与政策研究支持；为中国的中东国家对外政策和 “一带一路”倡议服务；为国家的叙利亚及中东研究培养专业人才。

自成立以来，中心在学术研究、国际交流、科研成果产出、咨政建言、人才培养和社会服务等方面取得了优异的成绩。

The Syria Research Center of Northwest University was approved as an international and regional research registration center by the Ministry of Education in July 2017, and was officially established on October 13, 2017. It is the only Syrian studies institution in the country to date. The nature and orientation of the Syria Research Center is to build a high-end, shared, and open platform with essential functions such as disciplinary construction, academic research, international exchanges, think tank, talent cultivation, and social services, and to conduct collaborative research on the Syrian issues and more; the Center is committed to building a high-end think tank on the Syrian issues in China and promoting the communication among China, Syria and other Middle Eastern countries by providing academic and policy research support. The Center also serves to cultivate talents for China's foreign policies in the Middle East, the "the Belt and Road" initiative and Syria and the Middle East studies.Since its establishment, the Center has made excellent achievements in academic studies, international communications, scientific research output, political advice, talent cultivation, and social service
