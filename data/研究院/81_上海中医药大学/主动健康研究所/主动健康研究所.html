主动健康研究所

【概况】

上海中医药大学、上海市中医药研究院主动健康研究所设立于上海中医药大学附属第七人民医院，是医院建设的首家上海市级研究所。依托上海市第七人民医院作为浦东新区北部区域医疗中心的功能定位，聚焦产学研深度融合，以数据驱动创新深入探索构建主动健康新体系。

办公室设在临床研究管理办公室，组成人员涵盖学科建设管理办公室、科研处等研究人员。现有含博士后、博士在内的专职科研人员10人，兼职PI与科研专家15人，国医大师3人，国家名中医1人，省市级名中医14人，在生物医疗领域的临床研发、新药创新、成果转化等方面，具备丰富的医疗资源与强大的学科基础。与上海中医药大学、上海理工大学、上海市生物医药研究院、苏州科技大学等多家科研院所建立了成熟的战略合作基础。

研究所紧密围绕上海中医药大学和上海市中医药研究院的建设目标，依托上海市第七人民医院牵头建设的SEVEN LAB一体化医产融合创新与转化中心，融合区块链、大数据、人工智能、云计算、物联网等新一代信息技术，持续加大对主动健康新体系的构建、人工智能赋能医工交叉、院内制剂和新药研发方向的支持力度。在数字技术的支撑下，研发基于云服务的科普平台，顺应健康中国战略的需要，建立大数据健康档案，构建主动健康服务规范标准体系，加速医工交叉多学科合作与主动健康服务的嵌入融合。深入探索大数据模型与图像识别的智慧装备研发，打造数智化中医药特色的医工交叉创新体系，共建生物医学科技创新孵化器等产业融合平台，赋能科技成果向临床应用转化。

The Institute of Active Health

Our responsibilities

The institute was officially renamed on March 20,2025.Our major responsibilities are as follows:

1. The Institute of Active Health of Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and Shanghai Research Institute of TCM is established at the Seventh People's Hospital Affiliated to Shanghai University of TCM. It is the first municipal-level research institute constructed by the hospital.

2.  The institute focuses on the deep integration of industry, academia, and research, and explores the construction of a new active health system through data-driven innovation.

Our achievements

1. The institute possesses abundant medical resources and a strong disciplinary foundation in clinical research and development, new drug innovation, and technology transfer in the field of biomedical science.

2. It has established a mature strategic cooperation foundation with multiple scientific research institutions, including Shanghai University of TCM, University of Shanghai for Science and Technology, Shanghai Institute of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, and Suzhou University of Science and Technology.

3.  It continuously increases support for the construction of a new active health system, the empowerment of medical engineering integration through artificial intelligence, and the research and development of hospital preparations and new drugs.

Our ongoing activities

1. Supported by digital technology, it develops a popular science platform based on cloud services, establishes a big data health file, accelerates the integration of multidisciplinary cooperation in medical engineering and active health services.

2. It deeply explores the research and development of intelligent equipment based on big data models and image recognition, jointly constructs industrial integration platforms such as biomedical science and technology innovation incubators to empower the transformation of scientific and technological achievements into clinical applications.

Our staff

The office is located in the Clinical Research Management Office, with staff members including researchers from the Office of Discipline Construction Management and the Department of Scientific Research. The institute currently has 10 full-time researchers, including postdoctoral fellows and PhDs, 15 part-time principal investigators and scientific research experts, 3 national TCM masters, 1 national famous TCM doctor, and 14 provincial and municipal famous TCM doctors.
