Basics

西南财经大学交子金融科技创新研究院的成立是西南财经大学贯彻落实国家创新驱动发展战略，服务国家、行业、区域经济社会发展的重大举措和深化改革、创新科研与社会服务体制机制的重大决策。成都西南财大交子金融创新研究院有限公司是研究院的运营实体，服务于学校中国特色世界一流学科建设，是在学校领导下集成、统筹金融科技创新资源，集管理、运营、服务为一体的综合性平台。

Jiaozi Institute of Fintech Innovation (JIFI) at SWUFE is established aiming to implement the national innovation-driven development strategy and to serve the social-economic development of the state, district and sector. Chengdu SWUFE Jiaozi Institute of Fintech Innovation Co., Ltd. is JIFI’s operating entity, surpporting the world-class discipline construction, and is a comprehensive platform integrating management, operation and services for coordinating fintech innovation resources.

                                                                                         
                                                                                       

科技引领 创新赋能

扎根学校 服务社会

我们以“科技引领，创新赋能，扎根学校，服务社会”为愿景，致力于发展成为国内一流、特色鲜明的金融科技高端人才集聚与培养基地、应用基础研究与交叉前沿研究的学术高地、科技成果转移转化示范基地以及国际交流与合作中心。

The company is the integrated operating entity of the university science and technology innovation platform, with a major role of stepping up overall planning to booster edge-cutting disciplinary construction. It also strives to build a top-level Fintech talents base, a high-end communication platform in the field at home and abroad, and becomes the highland of applied fundamental research and cross-disciplinary research.

 

主营业务

MAIN BUSINESS

我们聚焦金融科技科创空间建设、成果转化全链条“一站式”服务、高端金融科技人才培养和金融科技创业投资等四大业务板块。平台现有大数据研究院、金融科技国际联合实验室、中国区块链研究中心、数据科学与人工智能研究中心等新型研发机构，研发人员120余人，其中正高职称人员31名，副高职称39人。

The companys business covers Fintech innovation space construction, single-point service for research transformation, high-end Fintech talents cultivation, Fintech VC investment, etc.. The platform now has several research centers such as Big Data Research Institute, Fintech Innovation Center, Blockchain Research Center of China, Institute for Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, etc..
