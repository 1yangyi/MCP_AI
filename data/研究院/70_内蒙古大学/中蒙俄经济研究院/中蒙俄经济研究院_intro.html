内蒙古大学中蒙俄经济研究院

　　

内蒙古大学中蒙俄经济研究院成立于2015年12月。设立的宗旨为：致力于打造服务国家向北开放的新型智库，在共建“一带一路”倡议框架下，进一步推动中蒙俄经济走廊建设和内蒙古国家向北开放重要桥头堡建设的深入研究，为国家和自治区党委政府和有关部门科学决策提供智力支持。

中蒙俄经济研究院以“一带一路”框架下中蒙俄经济走廊建设为主题，聚焦了五个优势和特色研究方向：（1）中蒙俄经济走廊建设研究；（2）“一带一路”倡议下内蒙古国家向北开放重要桥头堡建设研究;（3）中蒙俄经济走廊建设背景下内蒙古制度型开放研究；（4）俄罗斯和蒙古国经济社会发展研究；（5）内蒙古参与东北亚区域合作研究。

中蒙俄经济研究院将充分发挥内蒙古大学学科齐全、人才密集和与俄蒙合作交流广泛的优势，组织国内外相关领域专家学者，围绕中蒙俄经济走廊建设和国家向北开放重要桥头堡建设的重大现实问题，积极开展多学科的综合研究，为国家和自治区党委政府和有关部门科学决策提出一系列具有针对性和可行性的政策建议。

The Center of Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Research of IMU was established in December 2015. The purpose of the establishment is: committed to building a new type of think tank serving the country's northward opening up, under the framework of the joint construction of the "the Belt and Road" initiative, further promote the in-depth research on the construction of the Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Corridor and of an important gateway for country's northward opening up of Inner Mongolia, and provide intellectual support for the scientific decision-making of party committees, governments, and relevant departments of the state and Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region.

The Center of Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Research focuses on the construction of the Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Corridor under the framework of the "the Belt and Road", focusing on five advantages and distinctive research directions: (1) Research on the construction of the Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Corridor; (2) Research on the construction of the important gateway for the country's northward opening up of Inner Mongolia under the "the Belt and Road" initiative; (3) Research on Inner Mongolia's institutional opening-up under the background of the construction of the Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Corridor; (4) Research on the Economic and Social Development of Russia and Mongolia; (5) Research on the Inner Mongolia participates in regional cooperation in Northeast Asia.

The Center of Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Research will fully leverage the advantages of IMU in terms of complete disciplines, dense talent, and extensive cooperation and exchanges with Russia and Mongolia. It will organize experts and scholars in relevant fields at home and abroad to actively carry out interdisciplinary comprehensive research on major practical issues related to the construction of the Sino-Mongolia-Russia Economic Corridor and the important gateway for the country's northward opening up country's northward opening up of Inner Mongolia. A series of targeted and feasible policy recommendations will be put forward for the scientific decision-making ofparty committees, governments, and relevant departments of the state and Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region.
